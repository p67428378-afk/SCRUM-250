# Project Features

## SCRUM-250 - Hospital Management System - Patient & Appointment Core Management

### Feature Summary
A comprehensive Hospital Management System that enables healthcare staff and patients to manage patient records, doctor availability, appointment bookings, and medical consultation notes.

### Key Features
- Patient Registration & Profile Management
- Doctor Scheduling & Availability Management
- Appointment Booking & Calendar Management
- Medical Consultations & EHR Record Keeping
- Role-Based Access Control & Audit Logging
